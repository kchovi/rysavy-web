About us

Family tradition, experience and reliability since 1990.

Petr Ryšavý – Autodoprava s.r.o. is a family-run haulage company based in Choceň, with a history dating back to 1990. Back then, Petr Ryšavý started with a single vehicle and gradually built a stable transport company.

Over more than 35 years on the market, we have gained extensive experience in domestic and international road freight transport. Our team currently consists of approximately 30 employees who take part in the day-to-day delivery of our services.

We specialise in transporting liquid food commodities in food-grade tankers, transporting goods in curtainside semi-trailers, and transporting smaller consignments in light commercial vehicles.

Our company also has its own premises in Dvořisko near Choceň, which provide a base for our fleet and its maintenance.

Despite gradual growth, we have kept the character of a family business and the values on which it was founded – a personal approach, flexibility, responsibility and reliability.

Our goal is to build long-term relationships with our customers and to be a partner they can always rely on.

Our history

2010

Fleet renewal

Refrigerated transport and fleet renewal

We bought our first refrigerated box and started running regular routes from the Czech Republic to Western Europe (the Benelux countries, England, Germany, France, Spain). The fleet was significantly renewed: we sold the Euro III emission class tractor units and replaced them with more environmentally friendly Euro V vehicles.

2008

Crisis

Economic crisis

The economic crisis brought a sharp drop in work. We deployed our trucks on transport within the European Union and carried goods mainly between the Netherlands and Italy and between Germany and Italy.

2005

Kogel

Exclusive carrier for the Kogel brand

As an exclusive carrier, we began transporting semi-trailer chassis (frames) of the Kogel brand on the Choceň – Burtenbach route. Another important part of our work was the export of machines for producing textiles and carpets from Litomyšl to Denmark.

2001

Own premises

Premises in Dvořisko and our own workshop

We bought premises in the village of Dvořisko near Choceň and adapted them for the needs of a haulage business. Since then, we have serviced and repaired our fleet ourselves.

1999

Tankers

Food-grade tankers

The number of tractor units grew to 12. We bought our first food-grade tanker and gradually more, until there were seven. We carried palm oil from Rotterdam or Dronten to Choceň for the company Agro-best, a manufacturer of animal feed. The tankers were fully utilised, with 14 to 16 tankers loaded every week.

1996

Agro-best

New vehicles and cooperation with Agro-best

We bought DAF and Volvo vehicles and began cooperating with Agro-best Choceň, a manufacturer of feed mixtures. We exported goods mainly to Croatia, Hungary, Slovakia and Germany.

1993

Export

First export transport

We bought a Škoda LIAZ vehicle and a BSS semi-trailer on lease. Our export destinations became Russia, Italy, Germany, Romania, Belgium and others. We mainly transported timber and garden sheds for large department stores all over Germany.

1990

Beginnings

From a wrecked Avia to the first route to Italy

We bought a wrecked Avia and repaired it ourselves. At first we delivered groceries, then we started driving to Italy for the company AudisBus (Rychnov nad Kněžnou) with spare parts and other components for buses.
